ABOUT RP SHIELD

RP Shield was created to give individuals and small business owners someone to call after the damage is done and tools to reduce the chance of it happening again.

Real Support, Backed by Experience

We’ve worked in cybersecurity long enough to know most people don’t need more tools, they need someone they trust. Just real support from someone who’s been there.
